What is Free Shipping?

At Cartify, we believe in making your shopping experience as seamless and affordable as possible. Our free shipping policy ensures that you can enjoy your favorite products without worrying about extra delivery costs. We handle the shipping fees so you can focus on what matters most - finding the perfect items for your needs.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum order value of $50 or more
  • Available for all registered Cartify customers
  • Applies to standard shipping methods
  • Valid for products sold and shipped by Cartify

Supported Locations

We currently offer free shipping to all addresses within the continental United States. This includes all 48 contiguous states. We're continuously working to expand our free shipping zones to serve more customers worldwide.

Exceptions

Please note that free shipping may not apply to:
